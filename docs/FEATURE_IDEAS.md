# Feature ideas for later

Saved at the user's request on 2026-10-24. These are ideas, not approved implementation work.

## First list

1. Profiles & Scenes: save complete customization setups; later add contextual switching.
2. Visual Theme Studio: edit surfaces in a live preview, with undo and before/after comparison.
3. Expanded edge-panel widgets: media, volume, brightness, timers, device information and remote PC actions.
4. Personal automation builder: user-defined triggers and actions.
5. Theme sharing and backup: export, preview imports and restore configurations.

Suggested sequence discussed: profiles, backup/sharing, then automation.

## Second list

6. Remote PC workspace: PC status, commands, files, media and downloads through Tailscale.
7. Notification workspace: grouping, snoozing, reminders and compact views.
8. Lock-screen dashboard: configurable weather, calendar, battery, media and PC-status widgets.
9. Gesture actions: screenshots, app pairs, shortcuts and remote commands.
10. Floating mini-tools: calculator, notes, clipboard, timers and translation.
11. Network dashboard: per-app traffic, connection history, latency and data usage.
12. Customization diagnostics: visibility explanations, apply latency and targeted recovery.

## Retained major candidate

Phone-PC continuity: stream phone apps to PC windows, share input/clipboard and move files/tasks between devices. Retained by the user as a potential candidate, not approved for implementation.
